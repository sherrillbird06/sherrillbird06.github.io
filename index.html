#IT-0101 Who is Jaylen Sherrill? NEW ▶

I'm an IT student at Nashville State Community College, but my path into IT started before I ever touched a textbook. I've spent the last two years in roles where I was the person people came to when something stopped working — a scanner that wouldn't read, a POS terminal throwing errors mid-transaction, a comms system dropping out during ground operations. Staying calm and fixing the problem while everyone around me was stressed is a pattern I noticed in myself, and it's what pushed me to make IT support an actual career instead of just a skill I happened to have.

What draws me to a role like this one specifically is the operational side — supporting people and systems that have to work correctly under real time pressure, not just a helpdesk in an office. My time as a ramp agent gave me a real feel for airport operations and the standards that come with working around aircraft, which I don't think many entry-level IT candidates can say.

Right now I'm building out the formal side: Microsoft 365/Azure AD fundamentals, scripting logic, and documentation practices. The two tools further down this page aren't class assignments — I built them from scratch to prove I can take a concept and actually ship something with it.
